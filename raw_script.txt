1. Imagine a laboratory where prisoners were turned into living death traps.  
2. That was Unit 731, Japan's hidden nightmare that turned science into pure horror.  
3. First up, the plague bomb tests - they dropped infected fleas from planes over Chinese towns, watching disease explode like fireworks.  
4. Even worse, they forced victims to inhale chlorine gas while strapped to tables, recording every gasp and seizure.  
5. They didn't stop at gases. They injected anthrax into soldiers and watched the flesh melt away in minutes.  
6. The so called vibrio cholerae experiments were a twisted race: they poured contaminated water down throats and timed how fast the victims drowned in their own vomit.  
7. Now the most chilling: they harvested organs from living subjects, stitching them back together to study how long a human could survive after a heart was ripped out.  
8. And the final horror - they let a plague infested corpse crawl through a schoolyard, then sent the same kids to the front lines, hoping the disease would be a secret weapon.  
9. All this was covered up by the US, which traded the data for silence and let the scientists walk free after the war.  
10. Which atrocity blows your mind the most, and why do you think the world still pretends it never happened?
